# Your task: name the subject of an article, as a blog category

The Festilaw blog is written for small makers and sellers, inside or outside the EU, who sell physical products to EU consumers. It deals with product safety, the GPSR, the responsible person, labelling and marketplaces. Its articles are filed in categories, one subject each.

Read the article below (its title, its excerpt and its section headings) and name its main subject, as the name of the category that should hold it.

- The main subject is what the article mainly explains: a role (such as the responsible person), a type of product (such as toys or cosmetics), an obligation (such as labelling or the technical file), a sales channel (such as marketplaces) or the checks made by the authorities.
- A category is named after one such subject, never after what every article of the blog deals with: "Product information", "Product safety", "GPSR" or "EU compliance" are too broad, since every article would fit.
- Give the English name of that subject in 1 to 4 words, in sentence case. It must be broad enough for future articles on the same subject, and never the topic of this article itself (write "Toys", not "Wooden toys on Etsy").
- You are not shown the categories the blog already has. Name the subject as the article shows it.
- "reason": one English sentence saying why this is the main subject of the article.
